06.4Validation & Review

The architecture is checked against the two sources it came from: the approved SRS and the prototype usability evaluation. Every view is traced back to the SRS features and requirement identifiers, each quality attribute scenario is given a measurable target taken from the SRS, and the trade-offs between those targets are recorded on the Architectural Style page. The log below tracks that review.
