01 // The Project

Problem Statement

People in Saudi Arabia who want to run, cycle or hike outdoors struggle to find suitable partners, stay motivated and access organized, professionally led group sessions. Existing fitness apps focus on individual tracking, and coach-led communities are rare and mostly reached by word of mouth.

Proposed Solution

Intaliq combines activity logging, personal goals, coach-managed group sessions, partner matching and an AI fitness chatbox in a single Arabic/English mobile app, backed by a cloud database, authentication, push notifications and an AI recommendation service.

System Purpose

To make outdoor fitness more accessible, motivating and social, in line with Vision 2030 goals for physical activity, while keeping core tasks a few taps away, protecting personal data, and meeting the SRS targets of 99% availability and results within 3 seconds.
