SEC_03

Workstreams

The SE322 work is split into six workstreams. The team works through them together, carrying the Intaliq architecture from the approved SRS to the final validated package.

WS-01

Architecture & Style

Selecting the client–server style, recording its trade-offs and keeping the architecture aligned with the SRS drivers.

WS-02

4+1 Views & UML

Modelling the user, logical, process, development and physical views, and drawing the UML diagrams behind them.

WS-03

Quality Attributes

Turning the SRS targets for performance, availability, security and usability into measurable scenarios.

WS-04

Design Patterns

Applying Observer, Repository and State to the session, data-access and booking parts of the system.

WS-05

Documentation

Writing the architecture and design documents and keeping this site in step with them.

WS-06

Validation & Presentation

Tracing the views back to the SRS, reviewing the trade-offs and preparing the final presentation.
